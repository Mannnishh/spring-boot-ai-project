New Hire Onboarding Checklist
Acme Robotics — Internal Policy Document
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

Day 1
- IT provisions the new hire's laptop and accounts before their start
  date, coordinated by the hiring manager.
- New hire orientation session runs at 9:00 AM on the first day and
  covers company policies, benefits, and org structure.

Week 1
- The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy to help the new hire
  navigate their first weeks.
- The new hire completes mandatory security and compliance training
  through the internal learning portal.

30-Day Mark
- The new hire's manager conducts a first performance check-in to
  discuss early progress and any blockers.
- IT performs an access review to confirm the new hire has access to
  exactly the systems required for their role, no more and no less.

90-Day Mark
- The new hire becomes eligible for the Remote Work Policy, provided
  their role is approved for remote work.
