Expense Reimbursement Policy
Acme Robotics — Internal Policy Document
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Submission Deadline
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase
date. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed except by
written exception from a department head.

2. Approval Threshold
Expenses under $150 are automatically approved on submission. Expenses
of $150 or more require explicit approval from the employee's manager
before payout is processed.

3. Travel Per Diem
Employees on approved business travel receive a per diem of $75 per day
to cover meals. The per diem is not reduced for partial travel days.

4. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment expenses, and traffic or parking fines
are not reimbursable under any circumstances, regardless of the amount
or approval level.

5. Reimbursement Turnaround
Once approved, expense reimbursements are paid out within 10 business
days via the employee's standard payroll method.
