Remote Work Policy
Acme Robotics — Internal Policy Document
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Eligibility
Employees become eligible to work remotely after completing 90 days of
continuous employment. Eligibility is granted by the employee's direct
manager and recorded in the HR system.

2. Equipment Stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time annual stipend of $500 to cover home
office equipment such as a monitor, chair, or desk. Stipend requests are
submitted through the Expense Reimbursement process.

3. Core Collaboration Hours
All remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in
their local time zone, so that meetings and pairing sessions can be
scheduled without excluding remote team members.

4. Communication Expectations
Employees are expected to respond to Slack messages within 4 business
hours during their working day. A weekly one-on-one meeting with the
employee's manager is required and may not be skipped for more than two
consecutive weeks without manager approval.

5. Review Cadence
Remote work arrangements are reviewed by the employee's manager every 6
months to confirm the arrangement is still working for both the employee
and the team.
